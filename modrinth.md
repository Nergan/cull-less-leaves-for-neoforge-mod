A NeoForge port of Cull Less Leaves for Minecraft 1.21.1. Inner leaf faces are culled and the outer layers stay. Client-side. The Fabric version is isXander's original mod, not this repository.

## NeoForge

Required: `culllessleaves-neoforge-1.21.1-1.4.3.jar` and Kotlin for Forge 5.8.0. Optional: Sodium for NeoForge 1.21.1, `0.6.13+mc1.21.1` or newer, including 0.8.x.

## Fabric

Install the original [Cull Less Leaves](https://modrinth.com/mod/cull-less-leaves). This project does not rebuild it.
